# Present Continuous Tense

## Video 1: How to Use the Present Continuous - English Verb Tenses Grammar Lesson (Oxford Online English)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0djfJqWhnpY
- **Durasi:** 12 menit 55 detik

### Materi Transkrip
1. **Struktur Kalimat Present Continuous**
   - **Positif (+):** Subjek + *be (am/is/are)* + *Verb-ing* (*"He's washing his car"*, *"They're watching a film"*).
   - **Negatif (-):** Tambahkan *not* setelah *be* (*"She isn't doing anything"*, *"I'm not working on that right now"*).
   - **Tanya (?):** Pindahkan *be* ke depan subjek tanpa menambah *do/did* (*"Is he doing anything?"*, *"Are you talking to me?"*, *"What are you watching?"*).
2. **7 Fungsi Penggunaan Present Continuous**
   - **Sedang terjadi saat ini (*Happening right now*):** Aktivitas yang berlangsung tepat saat berbicara (*"He's washing his car"*).
   - **Sudah dimulai tetapi belum selesai (*Started but not finished*):** Berlangsung di sekitar waktu sekarang, meski tidak dilakukan tepat detik ini (*"I'm reading a good book at the moment"*, *"She's looking for a new apartment"*).
   - **Mendeskripsikan gambar atau foto:** Menggambarkan momen di dalam gambar (*"They are sitting on a beam high above the city"*).
   - **Situasi sementara (*Temporary situations*):** Berbeda dari kebiasaan permanen (*"He's living with his parents"* vs permanen *"He lives with his parents"*).
   - **Situasi yang sedang berubah (*Changing situations*):** Perkembangan bertahap (*"The population of our city is growing by around 5% a year"*, *"My English is slowly getting better"*).
   - **Rencana pasti di masa depan (*Future arrangements*):** Rencana sosial atau jadwal yang sudah ditetapkan waktu/tempatnya (*"We're meeting outside the cinema at 8:00"*, *"They're coming to ours for dinner on Saturday"*).
   - **Kebiasaan yang mengganggu/aneh (*Annoying/strange repeated actions*):** Wajib menggunakan kata keterangan seperti *always, constantly,* atau *continually* (*"He's always forgetting to bring the things he needs"*).
   - **Perasaan yang baru mulai disadari:** (*"I'm realizing I made the wrong decision"*).

## Video 2: Present Continuous | An Easy English Grammar Lesson (English Panda)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QqxdZzOorAU
- **Durasi:** 4 menit 25 detik

### Materi Transkrip
1. **3 Komponen Pembentuk Present Continuous**
   - **Subject + Be Verb (am/is/are) + Main Verb-ing**
   - Contoh: *"I am drinking coffee"*, *"We are learning Spanish this semester"*, *"They are having a party on Saturday."*
2. **3 Kegunaan Utama dalam Percakapan Sehari-hari**
   - **Aksi yang terjadi sekarang (*Actions happening right now*):** (*"I am speaking English"*, *"You are watching this video"*, *"She is studying for her exam"*).
   - **Aksi atau situasi sementara (*Temporary actions or situations*):** Berlangsung selama beberapa hari, minggu, atau bulan, tetapi tidak permanen (*"I'm staying at my friend's house this week"*, *"She is living in New York for a few months"*, *"We are saving money to buy a house"*).
   - **Rencana dan pengaturan masa depan (*Future plans and arrangements*):** Rencana yang sudah diputuskan (*"I am meeting my friend tomorrow"*, *"We are going to Hawaii next winter"*, *"I am going to the dentist at 3:00 p.m."*).
