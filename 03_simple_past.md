# Simple Past Tense

## Video 1: Learn English: Simple Past Tense - English Grammar (Learn on the Go)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmCE2Ip6XCI
- **Durasi:** 8 menit 54 detik

### Materi Transkrip
1. **Pengertian & Fungsi Simple Past Tense**
   - Digunakan untuk menunjukkan tindakan yang telah selesai dilakukan di masa lampau (*completed in the past*).
   - Penanda waktu lampau: *yesterday, last week, two years ago, in 2001,* atau bahkan *the previous minute*.
2. **Struktur Kalimat Positif (+)**
   - **Pola:** Subjek + *Verb 2 (Past Tense)* + Objek.
   - Bentuk kata kerja lampau tetap sama untuk semua subjek (*I, you, he, she, it, we, they worked late yesterday*).
   - **Regular Verbs (Kata Kerja Beraturan):** Ditambahkan akhiran **-ed** (*work → worked*, *pass → passed*, *type → typed*, *brush → brushed*).
   - **Irregular Verbs (Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan):** Berubah bentuk secara khusus (*make → made*, *run → ran*, *eat → ate*, *go → went*, *have → had*).
3. **Struktur Kalimat Negatif (-)**
   - **Pola:** Subjek + *did not (didn't)* + *Verb 1 (bentuk dasar)* + Objek.
   - **Aturan Penting:** Tidak boleh ada dua kata kerja bentuk lampau dalam satu klausa. Karena *did* sudah berbentuk lampau, kata kerja utama kembali ke bentuk dasar (*"I did not work till 8 pm yesterday"*, *"I didn't sleep well"*).
4. **Struktur Kalimat Tanya (?)**
   - **Pola:** *Did / Didn't* + Subjek + *Verb 1 (bentuk dasar)* + Objek?
   - Contoh: *"Did you work till 8 pm yesterday?"*, *"Didn't you sleep well?"*, *"Did mom go out?"*

## Video 2: Past Simple | An Easy English Grammar Lesson (English Panda)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kgcd4gj9zNw
- **Durasi:** 6 menit 17 detik

### Materi Transkrip
1. **Contoh Percakapan Aktivitas Kemarin**
   - Penggunaan *Past Simple* dalam cerita sehari-hari: *"I worked on a video"*, *"I studied for a test"*, *"I went shopping"*, *"We ate a really nice lunch"*, *"I played video games."*
2. **Aturan Ejaan Regular Verbs (-ed)**
   - Umumnya cukup tambahkan **-ed** (*watch → watched*, *work → worked*, *play → played*).
   - **Aturan Konsonan-Vokal-Konsonan (CVC):** Jika tiga huruf terakhir kata kerja beraturan berpola konsonan-vokal-konsonan, gandakan huruf konsonan terakhir sebelum menambah **-ed** (*stop → stopped*).
3. **Irregular Verbs (Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan)**
   - Tidak mengikuti aturan *-ed* dan harus dihafalkan bentuknya (*go → went*, *eat → ate*, *take → took*, *do → did*, *see → saw*, *write → wrote*).
4. **Kalimat Negatif & Tanya**
   - **Negatif:** Gunakan *didn't* + kata kerja dasar (*"I didn't do my homework last night"*, *"She didn't go to the party"*, *"We didn't eat lunch today"*).
   - **Tanya:** Gunakan *Did* + subjek + kata kerja dasar (*"Did you see the new movie?"*, *"Did they run in the race?"*, *"Did she write a letter to her friend?"*).
